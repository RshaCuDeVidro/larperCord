Built-in themes

Generated from themes/*.kdl. Every theme has a light and a dark mode, and the options below show up in Settings → Appearance.

Themes made by other people are in the community gallery, and Sharing your theme explains how to add yours.

#classico

The familiar layout, in your wallpaper's colors, with a server banner.

OptionDefaultWhat it does
Server banner (banner)onA colored strip with the server name above the channels.
Subtle texture (textura)onA very fine grain on the surfaces, so they don't look flat.
Channel tabs (abas)offShows open channels as tabs above the conversation.
CRT effect (crt)offSubtle scanlines over everything.

Source: classico.kdl · open it in the theme builder

#vidro

Glass cards over the wallpaper, with a sheen. On Linux your compositor blurs what's behind.

OptionDefaultWhat it does
Card sheen (brilho)onA strip of light at the top of each card, like real glass.
Aurora backdrop (aurora)onA curtain of light in the theme colors behind the cards. Still: generated once.
Channel tabs (abas)onShows open channels as tabs above the conversation.
Server banner (banner)offA strip with the server name above the channels.
Opaque background (opaco)offNo transparency: solid cards (lighter). The best look on Windows, which has no blur.
CRT effect (crt)offSubtle scanlines over the glass.

Source: vidro.kdl · open it in the theme builder

#terminal

tmux + IRC: monospace, channel tree, CRT effect and ASCII logo.

OptionDefaultWhat it does
CRT effect (crt)onScanlines and dark corners, like an old monitor.
ASCII logo (ascii)onThe app name drawn in characters above the channels.
Green phosphor (verde)offSwaps amber for the green of classic terminals (dark mode).
Graticule (reticula)onThe fine oscilloscope grid behind the text.
Radar backdrop (radar)offA still phosphor radar behind the windows.
Topographic map (mapa)offContour lines behind the windows.
Channel tabs (abas)onShows open channels as tabs at the bottom.

Source: terminal.kdl · open it in the theme builder

#subsolo

Underground terminal: fossil black, acid phosphor, ultraviolet and scanlines.

OptionDefaultWhat it does
Tunnel map (tuneis)onStill phosphor contour lines behind the panels.
Scanlines (crt)onScanlines and vignette, like a CRT.
Ultraviolet (roxo)offSwaps acid green for neon purple (deep web).
ASCII logo (ascii)onThe app name drawn above the channel list.
Top bar (topo)onBack/forward, search and help at the top.
Channel tabs (abas)onShows open channels as tabs at the bottom.

Source: subsolo.kdl · open it in the theme builder

#orbital

Ship console: graphite, mint, system numbering and servers in the bottom strip.

OptionDefaultWhat it does
Starfield (estrelas)onStill stars behind the panels (generated once, costs nothing).
Orbit system (orbitas)onThe orbit diagram in the background.
Moving background (movimento)offStars and moons drift very slowly, at 10 frames per second. Turns itself off with "Reduce motion" and when the window is in the background.
Top telemetry (telemetria)onConnection, ping, RAM and clock in the top bar.
Channel tabs (abas)onOpen channels as tabs above the conversation.
Search bar (topo)offShows the search/help bar below the wordmark (Ctrl+K still works).
Console scanlines (crt)offA subtle ship-screen effect over everything.

Source: orbital.kdl · open it in the theme builder

#cupertino

Mac-style desktop: wallpaper, floating window with traffic lights, translucent sidebar and a magnifying Dock.

OptionDefaultWhat it does
Bubbles (baloes)onThe conversation in bubbles, like a messaging app.
Dock (dock)onServers in the Dock, with magnification. Off, they move to the sidebar.
Moving wallpaper (movimento)offThe hills drift very slowly, at 10 frames per second. Turns itself off with "Reduce motion" and when the window is in the background.
Channel tabs (abas)offOpen channels as tabs above the conversation.
Real transparency (transparente)offRemoves the wallpaper: yours shows behind the window, blurred by your compositor on Linux.

Source: cupertino.kdl · open it in the theme builder
